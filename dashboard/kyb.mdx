Use KYB to submit business verification information for your merchant account.

## What to prepare

- Registered business name and registration details.
- Business website, product description, and operating region.
- Business registration documents when required.
- Beneficial owner or authorized representative information when requested.

## Review tips

- Keep business names consistent across documents, website, and account profile.
- Make sure your website clearly describes what customers buy.
- Respond to review follow-up requests with complete information.
