Use KYC to submit personal identity verification information when Kyren Pay requires it for account review or settlement access.

## What to prepare

- Legal name that matches your identity document.
- Identity document details and required images.
- Contact information that Kyren Pay can use for follow-up.
- Any additional information requested during review.

## Review status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not submitted | You have not submitted KYC information yet. |
| Pending | Kyren Pay is reviewing your submission. |
| Approved | Your personal verification has been approved. |
| Rejected | Review failed or more accurate information is needed. |
