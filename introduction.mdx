## From Checkout to a confirmed order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your merchant account">
    Review onboarding, enabled [payment methods](/dashboard/payment-methods), and [API credentials](/authentication) before starting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a product and Checkout Session">
    Define your product, price, and currency. Create the session on your server and redirect the customer to its Checkout URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and verify Webhooks">
    Set your notification URL before the first payment. Verify signatures against the original request body and handle duplicate events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment and fulfill the order">
    Use verified payment events and order records to update your system. A browser redirect alone is not proof of payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a complete implementation, see [Collect your first payment](/guides/collect-first-payment) and the [go-live checklist](/go-live-checklist).

## Payment methods and availability

Available payment methods, currencies, and limits depend on your merchant account, bound channels, and platform configuration. Check [Payment methods](/dashboard/payment-methods) and the [supported currencies API](/api-reference/config/get-supported-currencies) for your integration. Fees and settlement conditions follow your merchant agreement.

## API base URL

| Environment | Base URL |
| --- | --- |
| Production | `https://api.kyrenpay.com` |
